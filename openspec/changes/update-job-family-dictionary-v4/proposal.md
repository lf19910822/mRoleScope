# Proposal

## Why

The V3 quality audit shows that all seven AI-application titles also match
`platform_infra`, solely because V3 treats bare `AI` or bare `agent` as a
platform signal. This also makes unrelated AI client, backend, and algorithm
titles acquire platform evidence and can change their primary family through
the established count and final-position rules. A bare reference to AI or an
Agent does not establish that the role is platform or infrastructure work.

## What Changes

- Publish immutable `deterministic-job-family-dictionary-v4` and retain V1,
  V2, and V3 assets and derived results for audit.
- Remove only the bounded bare aliases `AI` and `agent` from
  `platform_infra`.
- Retain all fifteen stable keys, every other V3 alias, literal matching,
  reviewed project masking, cross-family evidence, and primary-family
  selection rules.
- Update the maintained readable latest dictionary and add V4 parser
  regressions for the narrowed platform boundary.

## Non-Goals

- No new family, JD-body input, AI/model call, schema migration, manual title
  override, source-sync change, score weighting, or primary-family priority
  change.

## Impact

The new dictionary version and digest make known title identities eligible for
the existing provider-free pending path. A separately authorized deployment
and historical catch-up derives V4 facts without contacting Qwen, Flash, or
another external model.
