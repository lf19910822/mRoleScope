# Proposal

## Why

The comparison established that C — 边注档案 — gives a recruiter or interviewer
the clearest reading structure for a real, long technical JD. Its margin index
provides orientation without pretending source requirements are a workflow;
its editorial rows retain density without returning to a stack of rounded
bubbles. The product owner has selected C as the production detail layout.

## What Changes

- Promote C to the normal JD detail route, without a preview query parameter.
- Preserve its exact selected composition: formal role brief and facts,
  complete metadata tags, a desktop margin index, source-faithful editorial
  rows, and its inline mobile directory.
- Keep the existing public Serving response, deterministic body presentation,
  and safe technology-highlight ranges. No fixture, parsing, API, or persisted
  data change is introduced.
- Apply C's selected contrast treatment in the shipped view: deeper Slate
  source prose and strong Sea Mint technology emphasis with a Lagoon underline.
- Remove the A/B alternatives, fixed preview switcher, keyboard preview logic,
  and preview-only route selection before release.

## Non-Goals

- It does not alter original source text, source order, job modelling, the
  Serving endpoint, or technology keyword matching.
- It does not reintroduce per-item cards, hover/tilt motion, a project-yellow
  technology treatment, or experimental controls into the selected layout.
